Just throw the ball & hit the curb. Nice and simple ;)

And then repeat. At least three times. In a row. And then repeat that six times on different levels to beat the game. Sounds easy, right?
Well then... just give it a try!

Curbs Minigame bases on a childs game usually called Curbs - rules are really simple. Stand on the one curb and throw the ball. If you hit the curb on the other side of the street - you've just earned yourself a point! But if you won't... well, I guess you'll have to start over. But no worries, it's just a pure, simple, frustration! If you manage to hit the curb three times you advance to different level.

Curbs Minigame is completely free to play game (no ads!) that takes few minutes to beat - it's a perfect game if you are currently waiting in queue. Just a few minutes of great fun with this game and you'll see time just flowing by. 
As long as the rules are simple, game itself isn't THAT easy. Because of that, your playtime may vary depending on both your skill and luck.

No playthrough is the same!
The game offers quite high amounts of replayability. Every single map out of total six is a bit different from the last one and what's more, in every playthrough sequence of levels on which you will be playing is completely randomized! 

Just give it a try, you'll love it ;) 
Enjoy!
